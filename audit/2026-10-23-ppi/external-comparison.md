The [validation scripts and six synthetic fixtures](../../inst/validation/external_ppi/) exercise a two-run design with separate run coefficients and compare the exported design against Nilearn 0.14.1 `OLSModel`/`ARModel`, NumPy 2.5.4 and SciPy 1.18.1. They check coefficients, residual variance, coefficient standard errors, named condition A-minus-B (equally across runs) estimate, standard error and t, and the two-condition F, including t and F contrast weights scaled by 1e-12. The script independently verifies condition contrast mapping and estimability. [Machine-readable errors](external-nilearn-results.json) retain all values. Maximum absolute errors across metrics were IID **2.15e-14**, four censored scans with IID **5.51e-14**, a duplicated column with estimable contrasts **5.69e-14**, fixed AR(1) **8.89e-15**, extreme column scales **4.32e-14**, and grouped AR **1.85e-13**, below 2e-8. For the extreme-scale design, the two run intercepts are scaled by 1e-8 and 1e8: raw NumPy numerical rank is 11, while column-equilibrated Nilearn fitting and multiPPI both report rank 12. The comparison maps coefficients and contrasts back to the original units; its coefficient and standard-error discrepancies are maximum relative errors with a unit floor, while other discrepancies remain absolute. For grouped AR, two operators estimated by multiPPI (phi **0.6844** and **-0.4586**) were held fixed for the Nilearn comparison; this checks conditional group-specific fitting and reassembly, not calibration of noise estimation. The estimator comparison is conditioned on the exported design: **it does not validate how either neural seed or interaction columns were constructed**, nor estimated-noise inference.

For the rank-deficient example, Nilearn 0.14.1 `OLSModel.fit` computes dispersion with `n - number_of_columns`, whereas this comparison uses `n - rank`. Nilearn's own unadjusted dispersion differed by up to 0.0129532; rank-corrected dispersion computed from its residuals was passed explicitly to Nilearn's contrast methods. Thus the reported rank-deficient t/F agreement is conditional on that external correction, not a claim that Nilearn's default rank-deficient result agrees. Aliased coefficient estimates are deliberately excluded from direct coefficient comparison and their nonestimability is independently checked.

Nilearn's `ARModel.whiten` leaves each first row unchanged. The fixed fmriAR plan uses exact AR(1) first-row scaling `sqrt(1 - phi^2)` at each run start. The oracle applies Nilearn's filter separately to each run, adjusts its first row, then fits with Nilearn `OLSModel`. Omitting the boundary correction changes the fitted coefficients by up to 0.0165384 in this fixture; the two default boundary conventions must not be treated as equivalent. Official implementation references: [Nilearn regression source](https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/blob/0.14.1/nilearn/glm/regression.py), [Nilearn contrast source](https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/blob/0.14.1/nilearn/glm/model.py), and [fmriAR source](https://github.com/bbuchsbaum/fmriAR).

## Actual SPM construction comparison

The real `spm_peb_ppi.m` from official SPM commit `54dc957191112dcad116e3eaa457e881fa8260de` was executed under GNU Octave 11.1.0 with synthetic `SPM` and `xY` structs and `showGraphics=0`. Its SHA-256 is `c28b73ca3e166ea065099bb0fc05a45969c22e1ba3400c8078cdff715b201615`. We retrieved the specific official dependencies, including the required matlabbatch classes, into `/tmp/multippi-spm`: 305 `.m` files totaling 934,183 bytes, all checked against the pinned tree's Git blob IDs and separately [hashed](external-spm-function-hashes.txt). The actual routine completed, produced `PPI_synthetic.mat`, and exported [synthetic PPI vectors and HRF](../../inst/validation/external_ppi/fixtures/) using the [probe](../../inst/validation/external_ppi/spm_octave_probe.m); the [execution receipt](external-spm-octave.log) records `SPM_PPI_SUCCESS 40`. Octave printed an `execution_exception` shutdown message despite exit status zero and a saved, readable result; it did not prevent calculation. No algorithm stubs or source patches were used. The absence of an Octave graphics toolkit did not prevent this `showGraphics=0` run.

The [comparison script](../../inst/validation/external_ppi/compare_spm.R) uses the same 40-scan raw physiological series, SPM-detrended task input, SPM HRF, TR=1, microtime=1 and sampling offset 0 in both current multiPPI constructions. It reports explicit `seed_adjust="none"` and the current default `seed_adjust="nuisance"`; the latter adjusts the seed for an intercept conditional on protected centered task variation. Task-convolution maximum absolute difference is **7.63e-17** for both. In the default path physiological-series maximum difference from SPM's adjusted series is **4.44e-16**, neural seed maximum difference **0.8506** (RMSE **0.1832**, correlation **0.9629**), and raw interaction maximum difference **0.07029** (RMSE **0.02325**, correlation **0.99916**); centering the interaction changes its maximum difference to **0.05682** (RMSE **0.01895**). In the explicit raw-seed path, the neural seed maximum difference is **1.2640** (RMSE **0.2242**, correlation **0.9475**) and raw interaction maximum difference **0.09844** (RMSE **0.03203**, correlation **0.99899**); centering the interaction gives **0.08165** maximum difference (RMSE **0.02727**). All metrics are retained in [the machine-readable receipt](external-spm-comparison.json). These are descriptive differences on one deliberately bounded synthetic specimen, not a numerical-equivalence or scientific-accuracy claim.

Important construction choices differ: SPM uses PEB deconvolution with its own covariance priors, a DCT basis padded by 128 samples, detrending of the neural seed and final interaction, and its `T0` sampling. The frozen multiPPI constructions use unpadded DCT ridge with lambda 0.1 and 40 basis functions; its neural seed is not centered and its convolution is a causal Riemann sum. The chosen scan-aligned microtime removes sampling-grid ambiguity for this example but does not equate the inverse problems. [SPM's PPI manual](https://github.com/spm/spm-docs/blob/main/docs/manual/ppi/ppi.md) and the [actual pinned function](https://github.com/spm/spm/blob/54dc957191112dcad116e3eaa457e881fa8260de/spm_peb_ppi.m) describe its construction.
