# Seven-scenario generative construction screen

The corrected 200-seed run completed with exit status 0 and all four prespecified gates passing. It supersedes the preserved `implementation-generative-initial-*` artifacts and `validate_ppi_generative-initial-script.R`; that initial run did not execute the complete recipe and its projection was not independent. A corrected three-seed pilot completed before the final run.

Executed on 2026-10-23 with R 4.5.1, aarch64-apple-darwin20, Apple Accelerate BLAS; 26 GiB free before the pilot. Source hashes were also unchanged: `ppi_design.R` `a710631460dac0ec6713b0a005d9d6d57a1e332bc1d3c67bb01de0f31d46f641`; `ppi_fit.R` `f9255db93f93bf855b4862a5cc7c4184fa303f78afd5b7c06b7c3cd886e7f930`. Simulation seed was 20261023 and bootstrap seed 20261024.

Each of 200 independent stationary neural AR realizations supplies ten target-noise draws, shared across scenarios. Runs have 90 and 110 scans, TR 2 s and microtime 4. Conditions are 10-second A/rest/B/rest blocks with a 10-second shift in run two. Nuisance is a run-period sine, and noisy-seed SD is one quarter of sampled neural-seed SD within each run. The script defines the remaining frozen signal amplitudes, HRF, fixed ridge, nuisance adjustment and IID fits. The independent generating operator is a dense causal sampled matrix; the delayed target HRF is shifted 1 s within the 0–32 s support. The same latent realization, seed-noise draw, and target-noise draws are paired across scenarios.

The target estimand `b*` is obtained independently using `stats::lm.fit` on the full constructed design and explicit equal-run A-minus-B interaction weights. The largest difference between that projection and the implementation's noiseless contrast was 2.39e-12. Confidence interval coverage is against this scenario-specific projection, not the generating amplitude 0.5. The mean relative target residual is the norm of the noiseless model residual divided by the norm of the run-centered target mean; it exposes mean misspecification and makes projection coverage descriptive under that misspecification.

Matched null maximum |b*| / maximum absolute target mean was 2.08e-16, below 1e-8. All designs had rank 14 and the contrasts were estimable; matched effect mean b* had a positive lower bootstrap limit; matched effect rejection exceeded the paired constant null by 0.4915, with paired seed-cluster bootstrap interval [0.4595, 0.5250]. The matched-effect b* was positive for all 200 seeds and its mean ratio to 0.5 was 1.335. These gates are bounded checks, not proof of general validity.

For each run and condition, true and constructed interaction columns are residualized against all noninteraction columns. The delayed scenario uses the shifted target HRF for its true interaction reference. Residualized oracle rank was 4 throughout. In the matched case, mean correlation was 0.7542, the minimum was -0.0071, mean relative Frobenius error was 0.6590 and maximum was 0.8428. Delayed and noisy-seed mean errors were 0.7181 and 0.6671, respectively. These adverse results remain visible despite effect detection; this screen does not establish accurate neural reconstruction.

The 5,000 fixed-seed bootstrap draws resample whole neural-seed clusters, preserving all ten target draws and pairing scenarios. Intervals describe variability over this simulated seed bank; 2,000 target outcomes are not independent new neural seeds and rates do not establish IID binomial calibration. Even the matched-effect model has a nonzero target-mean residual (relative norm 0.0534), so its 95.65% coverage of b* is empirical coverage under mean misspecification, not proof of exact nominal t-interval coverage. The delayed and noisy-seed models have larger target-mean residuals. Coverage against any of these projections does not establish coverage of the generating amplitude. The reported 0.6590 is relative residualized-interaction error, not neural-seed reconstruction error. The two matched nulls are algebraically equivalent after main-effect adjustment and share target-noise draws; identical 94/2000 rejections are expected, not two independent confirmations of calibration. No conclusion is made about estimated temporal noise, censoring, population data, or other designs.
